  deliverRecordedData; decoded speech is pulled at real-time pace in 10 ms

  coordinator reconnects with a seeded one. If WebRTC can't connect within

  (LiveTransportChoice); a refusal from the service (bad key) is not
  retried. The old LiveSocketWarmer now warms the HTTPS connection instead
  of a socket while on WebRTC.

  OPENCODE_LAB_VOICE_INPUT (a WAV in place of the mic),
